The hanger is a flat pear-shaped steel plate standing upright: a round upper end with a bolt passing through its centre, a hexagonal nut and a round washer tightened on that bolt, and a round lower end pierced by one large hole. Through that lower hole a carabiner is clipped and hangs straight down, drawn as an accurate offset-D carabiner with its frame, its straight gate on the right side and the hinge rivet at the bottom of the gate; below it hangs the short flat sewn sling of a quickdraw with its stitched end. Everything man-made keeps every edge, material thickness and moving part in its correct place. No climber, no rope, no perspective, no texture.
