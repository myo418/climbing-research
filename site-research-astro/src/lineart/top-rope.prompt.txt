--- 描くもの（ここを書き換えて描き直す）---
An indoor climbing gym top-rope wall seen from the side, the whole wall from the floor to the top inside the frame.
The wall is a flat panel about ten metres high with bolt-on plastic holds of different shapes screwed to it in a
scattered route, the panel seams and the grid of small mounting holes visible, and thick landing mats on the floor
at its base.
At the top of the wall the rope passes through a fixed anchor: a steel plate bolted to the top of the panel with
two screwgate carabiners hanging from it. Both carabiners are drawn at their true size — a carabiner is about as
long as a hand is wide, much smaller than a person's head — so they read as small hardware, not as large rings.
One strand of rope runs from the anchor down to a climber partway up the wall, tied in at the harness; the other
strand runs down the other side to a belayer standing on the mat, holding the rope through a belay device at the
waist, with the slack rope coiled on the mat.
Both figures full body. No face. Indoor gym only: no natural rock anywhere.
